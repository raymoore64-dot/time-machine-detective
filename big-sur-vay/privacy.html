Big Sur:Vay Privacy Policy

For the Big Sur:Vay iPhone app, published by Raymond Moore.

On-device records

Big Sur:Vay stores the audits, evidence summaries, follow-ups and setup information you enter on your device. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking, or developer-operated cloud. Your selected interface language stays on the device. Use de-identified process details and leave out patient identifiers and clinical records.

Optional wording suggestions

On supported devices, Apple’s on-device model supplies wording suggestions when you request one. You review a suggestion before applying it.

Exports and sharing

You choose whether to export and where to save or share each file. Backups and action handoffs use password protection. PDF reports and facility packs can be read by anyone with access to the exported file. The destination or transfer service you choose applies its own policies.

Retention and deletion

You can delete individual audits in the app. Uninstalling it removes its local records. Device backups exclude the local audit archive. An encrypted backup you create can be used to recover records. Manage exported copies at the destinations where you saved them.

Privacy questions

Email dialinnstudio26@protonmail.com. If you contact support, the publisher receives what you send and uses it to respond. Please omit passwords, patient details and audit files.
