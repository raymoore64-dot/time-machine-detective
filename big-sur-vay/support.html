Big Sur:Vay Support

Help with audits, follow-ups, encrypted handoffs, and backups.

Get started

In the app, open Home → How to use for the offline guide. Choose English or Arabic from Home → Language. Use Open sample audit to practice with fictional data.

Recover your work

Open Home → Backup and restore to preview and restore an encrypted backup. You’ll need that backup’s password. Before replacing your phone or removing the app, create and safely store an encrypted backup.

Contact support

Email dialinnstudio26@protonmail.com. Include your app version, iOS version and the steps that led to the issue. Please omit audit files, passwords, patient details and other sensitive information.
