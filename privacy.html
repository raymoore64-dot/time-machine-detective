Privacy policy

The Time Machine Detective is an iPhone historical investigation game. It has no app account, advertising, or developer-operated analytics. The app does not send your investigation notes, progress, saved findings, or archive queries to the developer.

Your cases and notes

The app saves case progress, pins, and notes on your device. Device backups managed by Apple may include this app data. You choose where to save exported debrief PDFs and how to share them. Deleting the app removes its local copy of your progress. Any PDFs you exported are managed separately in the destination you selected.

Purchases

Apple processes and restores purchases through the App Store. The app checks whether you have access to the purchased collection. The developer does not receive your payment card information through the app. Apple’s App Store and Privacy notice

Optional evidence assistant

On supported devices, the optional suggestion mode uses Apple’s on-device language model. The archive’s standard keyword search is available without that mode.

External sources and support

Opening a citation takes you to a third-party site in your browser. That site has its own privacy practices. If you email support, the operator receives your email address and the information you choose to share so they can respond.
