The Time Machine Detective support

For help with Case Files, a purchase, or a source citation, email dialinnstudio26@protonmail.com. Include the case title, app version, and iOS version if you know them.

Restore Volume One. Open either locked case in Case Files, then select Restore purchases. Use the Apple Account that originally purchased the collection.

Resume an investigation. Tap Continue in Case Files to reopen the most recent investigation. To switch cases, return to Case Files and tap a case.

Save a debrief. Open Board → Open case debrief → Export PDF and choose a save location.

Open an archival citation. Citations open in your browser and may require internet access. If a source has moved, include the case and citation title in your message.
